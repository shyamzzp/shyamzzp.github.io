Transport

Getting there / back

  • Fly, not train. Hyd↔Kerala train = 24–28h each way.
  • Open-jaw: IN → Calicut (CCJ). OUT → Kochi (COK). ~1h20m/leg.
  • Aug 31: COK 07:30 → Hyd ~09:15 (back by 10am). Red-eye night of Aug 30 = safer buffer.

Within Kerala

  • Car + driver, whole trip. ~₹2500–3500/day. One operator, every leg.
  • No self-drive — hairpin ghats + monsoon.
  • Alt mode: one backwater houseboat night (Aug = off-season).
